# NearMe — App Features Guide

> Plain-English overview of every screen and feature in the NearMe app. Written for anyone — no technical background needed.

---

## What is NearMe?

NearMe is a real-time social discovery app. It shows you a grid of real people who are physically nearby, lets you chat with them, see what they're up to right now, and connect through audio or video calls — all in one place.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

When you first open the app you go through a short onboarding flow:

1. **Welcome screen** — choose to create a new account or log in.
2. **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** — read and agree to both before continuing.
3. **Feature intro carousel** — a quick swipeable tour of what NearMe offers.
4. **Sign-up / Log in** — enter your email and password, or tap "Continue with Google" for one-tap sign-in via your Google account.
5. **Phone verification** — enter your phone number; you receive a one-time code (OTP) by SMS to confirm it's yours.
6. **Profile setup** — fill in your display name, a short bio, and upload at least one photo. You also pick a few profile attributes (body type, what you're looking for, etc.).

Once setup is complete, the app takes you straight to the main Browse grid.

### Logging Back In

On every return visit the app automatically restores your session — you won't be asked to log in again unless you tap **Log Out** in Settings or your session expires.

## Main Navigation

At the bottom of the screen are five tabs. The active tab is highlighted in orange; inactive tabs appear in a muted colour.

| Tab | Icon | What it does |
|-----|------|-------------|
| **Browse** | Grid squares | See nearby people in a photo grid |
| **Right Now** | Droplets | See what people nearby are up to right now |
| **Interest** | Heart | Who viewed you and who tapped you |
| **Inbox** | Chat bubble | Your conversations |
| **Store** | Diamond | Plans and add-ons |

---

## Browse

The home tab. Shows a grid of profile photos for people who are near you and were active in the last 14 days.

**What you see on each card:**
- Profile photo (tapping opens the full profile)
- Online status dot (green = online now)
- Verified badge if the person has completed phone + face verification
- Distance from you

**Actions from Browse:**
- Pull down to refresh the grid
- Tap a card to open that person's full profile
- Tap the **filter icon** (top-right) to narrow results

**Filters available:**
- Distance range
- Age range
- What they're looking for
- Body type
- Online now only

> Free accounts can interact with up to 20 unique people over their lifetime. When that limit is reached, the app shows an upgrade prompt instead of an error.

---

## Right Now

Shows you a live feed of people nearby who have posted what they're doing at this moment — like "grabbing coffee", "going for a walk", or "hosting tonight".

**Reading the feed:**
Each card shows the person's photo, display name, distance, their Right Now status text, and the category it falls under. Cards are sorted by distance (closest first) and appear at the top of the screen.

**Filter chips (top of screen):**
- **Distance** — sort by how close they are
- **Hosting** — show only people who are hosting at their place
- **Position** — filter by position preference

**Posting your own Right Now status:**
Tap the **"+ What are you up to?"** button at the top. A sheet slides up where you:
1. Write a short status (e.g. "Going for a run in the park")
2. Pick a category: Drinks, Coffee, Workout, Hangout, or Other
3. Choose how long it should stay visible: 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, or Tonight
4. Toggle **Hosting** on if you're at your place and open to visitors
5. Tap **Post** to publish

Your status disappears automatically when the chosen time is up. You can also clear it early by tapping your status and choosing Remove.

**Empty state:** If no one nearby has an active status, you'll see a prompt encouraging you to be the first to post.

---

## Interest

Split into two sub-tabs:

### Views
Who has visited your profile. Requires a **Gold plan or higher** — free and Premium users see a blurred/locked version and are prompted to upgrade.

Each row shows the person's photo, name, distance, and how long ago they viewed you.

### Taps
People who have tapped you (equivalent to a "like"). You can tap them back — a mutual tap opens the door to messaging.

Each row shows the person's photo, name, distance, and when they tapped you. Tap the **Tap back** button to return a tap, or tap their photo to view their full profile.

---

## Inbox

Your list of all conversations. Each row shows:
- The other person's profile photo
- Their display name and distance
- The last message in the conversation (truncated)
- A timestamp
- An unread badge (orange dot) if there are messages you haven't seen yet

Tap any conversation to open the chat screen.

## Store

Where you manage your plan and add-ons.

### Plans

| Plan | Who it's for |
|------|-------------|
| **Free** | Browse and chat with up to 20 people; limited call minutes |
| **Premium** | More interactions, no lifetime cap |
| **Gold** | Everything in Premium + see who viewed you |
| **Platinum** | All features, highest limits, AI features, travel mode |

### Add-ons (available to all paid plans)
- **Extra audio call minutes** — top up beyond your plan's included minutes
- **Extra video call minutes** — same for video

Purchases go through Razorpay (India's payment gateway). You'll see a secure payment sheet inside the app.

## Profile Detail

Tap any person's photo from Browse, Right Now, or Interest to open their full profile. You'll see:

- Their photo gallery (swipe through multiple photos)
- Display name, age, distance
- Verified badge (shown when both phone and face are confirmed)
- Bio
- Profile attributes (body type, position, what they're looking for, etc.)
- Right Now status if they have one active

**Actions on a profile:**
- **Tap** — send them a tap
- **Message** — start or continue a conversation
- **Audio call** — voice call (button always visible; active once the person allows calls)
- **Video call** — video call (same behaviour)
- **Block** — removes them from your grid and prevents contact
- **Report** — flag for review

---

## Chat

A one-to-one messaging screen.

**Features:**
- Send text messages in real time
- Message delivery states: sending → sent → delivered → read (blue tick)
- Tap the **phone icon** to start an audio call
- Tap the **video icon** to start a video call
- Call buttons are always visible in chat and become active once the recipient has calls enabled

---

## Calls (Audio & Video)

NearMe supports live audio and video calls powered by Agora.

**Starting a call:** Tap the phone or camera icon in any chat, or on a profile.

**Receiving a call:** An incoming call sheet pops up over whatever screen you're on — you can answer or decline without leaving what you were doing.

**Call time (free users):**
- Free accounts get **5 audio minutes** and **2 video minutes** per day
- A live countdown shows how many minutes remain during the call
- When time runs out, the call ends automatically
- Buy add-ons in the Store to get more minutes

**Paid users** have higher (or no) limits depending on their plan.

---

## Travel Mode (Explore)

Lets you virtually "move" your location so you can browse people in a different city or neighbourhood.

Open **Explore** from the Browse screen (compass icon, top-right). Pick a location on the map or search by city name. Once set, the grid shows people near that pinned location instead of your real one.

Available on **Platinum** plans.

---

## Filters

Accessed from the Browse tab (filter icon, top-right). Opens a full-screen panel where you adjust:
- Maximum distance
- Age range
- What they're looking for
- Body type
- Online now only toggle

Filters apply immediately when you close the panel and refresh the grid.

---

## Settings

Open from any profile-related screen (gear icon). Organised into sections:

**Account**
- View and edit your profile details
- Change email or password

**Safety**
- **Stealth mode** — your profile won't appear in anyone else's grid
- **Verified users only** — only show and be seen by people who are verified
- **Proximity shrink** — shows your distance as a rough range instead of an exact figure

**Appearance**
- **Dark mode / Light mode** toggle — switches the whole app between the dark "Golden Hour" colour palette and a lighter version

**Support**
- Help and feedback links

**Account actions**
- Log out

---

## Edit Profile

Reached from Settings → Edit Profile. Lets you update everything about how you appear to others:

- **Primary photo** — the main photo shown in the grid
- **Additional photos** — up to several extra photos visible on your profile
- **Display name**
- **Bio** — a short free-text description
- **Profile attributes** — body type, position, what you're looking for, and other fields

All photo uploads are handled securely — the app never exposes the raw storage path; photos arrive as time-limited secure links.

---

## Photo Albums

Private albums you can share selectively with specific people.

- **Create an album** — group photos together under a title
- **Grant access** — choose which people can see a particular album
- **Revoke access** — remove someone's access at any time
- **Viewing** — when someone grants you access, their album appears in your inbox or on their profile

---

## Verification

Adds a **verified badge** to your profile, building trust with other users.

There are two steps, and you need both to be considered fully verified:

1. **Phone verification** — confirms your phone number via OTP (done during onboarding; can be redone from Settings)
2. **Face / selfie verification** — you take a selfie that is compared against your profile photo to confirm you're a real person and that your photos are genuine

Once both are done, your profile shows a verified badge everywhere it appears in the app.

## App-Wide Features

These work in the background across every screen:

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Light & Dark theme** | The entire app adapts to your preferred colour scheme |
| **Offline banner** | A banner appears at the top when you lose internet; disappears when you reconnect |
| **Incoming call overlay** | An incoming call sheet appears no matter what screen you're on — you never miss a call |
| **Error recovery** | If something crashes, an error boundary catches it and shows a recovery option instead of a blank screen |
| **14-day activity filter** | Only people active in the last 14 days appear in Browse and Right Now — inactive accounts are hidden automatically |
| **Free tier cap** | Free users can interact with up to 20 unique people over their account's lifetime; hitting the cap shows an upgrade prompt |
| **Secure media** | All photos and videos are served as short-lived signed links — raw storage paths are never shown to users |
| **Session persistence** | Your login is remembered securely on-device; the app silently refreshes your session in the background |
| **Auto logout on session failure** | If your session can't be refreshed (e.g. revoked remotely), the app safely logs you out and returns to the login screen |
