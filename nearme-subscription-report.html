Live & verified

  • Chat Packs correctly add 5 / 15 / 35 slots on top of the free lifetime cap.
  • Travel Pass unlocks travel mode for every plan, not just Gold+.
  • New plan permissions apply instantly after checkout — no wait for entitlements to refresh.
  • Unsend (Premium: before read · Gold+: anytime) and Edit message (Gold+, 5-min window).
  • Call history included from Premium up, matching the published pricing.
  • Verified badge auto-granted on Gold/Platinum activation, retained after downgrade.
  • Boost & Spotlight now rank by tier strength instead of all five acting identically.
  • Featured Nearby carousel on Browse for active Spotlight holders.
  • Self-serve Cancel Subscription in the Store, with a plain-language confirmation step.
  • In-app feature comparison matrix and annual savings badges.
  • Platinum AI suite — icebreakers, reply suggestions, compatibility, Daily Top 10, profile optimizer — all live and correctly gated.
  • In-chat translation — live for Premium and up.

Known gaps

  • Boost tiers differ in ranking strength, not yet in radius or targeting reach.
  • Verified Badge add-on still requires a manual document-review step to activate.
  • Scheduled calls work end-to-end but aren't yet restricted to Gold+ specifically.
  • Advanced filters (education, occupation, etc.) are accepted by the API but don't yet affect results.
  • Free-tier photo caps and grid advertising, both mentioned in the original brief, are not yet built.
